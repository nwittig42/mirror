# GEO / AEO case studies and evidence (compiled 12 October 2026)

Reliability labels used throughout:
- **[IND]** independent research (party does not sell GEO services, or academic)
- **[1P]** first-party: published by the company that got the result
- **[V-NAMED]** vendor or agency published, customer named
- **[V-ANON]** vendor or agency published, customer anonymous
- **[V-SELF]** vendor or agency reporting on its own site (it sells the service it is describing)

"Opened" means the page stating the numbers was fetched and read during this research. Anything not opened is listed under Gaps, not as a finding.

## 1. What named-company case studies exist where GEO / AEO work led to a measured result?

### Takeaway
There are 20+ traceable cases, but almost all are B2B SaaS, fintech or ecommerce, and almost all are published by the vendor that sold the tool or service. The strongest quotable ones are first-party statements from Vercel, Tally, Webflow, Docebo and Ahrefs (share of signups or leads coming from AI), plus Profound's named customer stories (Ramp, Plaid, Arizona College of Nursing, MongoDB, WHOOP).

### Cited Findings

**First-party (the company itself reports the number)**

- **Vercel** (developer cloud platform, large) [1P]. Vercel's own blog (10 June 2025, Kevin Corbett and Malte Ubl) says ChatGPT refers about 10% of new Vercel signups, up from 4.8% the previous month and 1% six months earlier. Metric: share of signups. Tactics listed: in-depth original content with data and code, clear heading hierarchy, Schema.org / JSON-LD, server-side rendering because most AI crawlers do not run JavaScript, Reddit / GitHub / Hacker News participation, content refresh at 30/90/180 days. Timeline: roughly 6 months from 1% to 10%. — [Vercel blog](https://vercel.com/blog/how-were-adapting-seo-for-llms-and-ai-search); CEO posts: [10% post](https://x.com/rauchg/status/1910093634445422639), [4.8% post, "less than 1% 6 months ago"](https://x.com/rauchg/status/1898122330653835656)
- **Tally** (form builder, bootstrapped, tiny team) [1P]. Blog post by co-founder Marie Martens, 10 June 2025: "ChatGPT became our #1 referral source"; over 2,000 new users signed up via AI tools in the preceding months (tracked only; onboarding survey suggests more). MRR went from $175K (Feb 2025, "$2M ARR") to $258K (June 2025, $3M ARR). Tally did NOT run a deliberate GEO campaign; it credits years of public presence in forums, Reddit and blogs, and the April 2025 ChatGPT browsing rollout. — [Tally blog](https://blog.tally.so/from-2-to-3m-arr-how-we-bootstrapped-tally-with-a-tiny-team/)
- **Webflow** (website builder, ~300,000 customers) [1P via interview]. Kyle Poyar's Growth Unhinged, 2 Nov 2025, interview with Webflow's VP of growth Josh Grant: 10% of signups come from AI discovery, growing 4x year on year; ChatGPT traffic converts at 24%, about 6x Google; ChatGPT is 91% of LLM referrals; about two in three LLM-referred conversions happen within 7 days. Tactics: tracks visibility with Profound, Reddit engagement as community members, core SEO fundamentals. They tested llms.txt and saw no significant lift. — [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/traffic-is-no-longer-reliable)
- **Docebo** (enterprise learning platform) [1P via interview]. Growth Unhinged, 8 Oct 2025: AI tools were the self-reported discovery source for 12.7% of high-intent leads in Q3 2025, up 429% in a year (about 5x Q3 2024). Docebo holds 25% share of voice on its category's top AI queries vs 18% for the next competitor. One person runs SEO and AEO. Tactics: mandatory "How did you hear about us?" field on the demo form, ~200 late-funnel "money keywords", share of voice tracked in Xfunnel. — [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/ai-discovery-playbook). HubSpot's AEO launch page (updated 14 Apr 2026) says Docebo "now gets nearly 15% of its leads from AI traffic" (no period given). — [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/company-news/hubspot-aeo)
- **Ahrefs** (SEO software) [1P]. Patrick Stox, 16 June 2025: AI search was 0.5% of Ahrefs' traffic over the last 30 days but drove 12.1% of signups; AI search visitors converted at 23x the rate of traditional organic search visitors. Single site, the author doubts it will scale. — [Ahrefs blog](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-traffic-conversions-ahrefs/)

**Vendor-published, customer named (Profound customer stories; none of these pages show a publication date; accessed 12 Oct 2026)**

- **Ramp** (fintech, enterprise) [V-NAMED]. AI visibility in the Accounts Payable category rose from 3.2% to 22.2% "within a month"; two new pages earned 300+ citations in one month; rank among fintech brands in the category moved from 19th to 8th. Actions: built two Accounts Payable software pages (small business, large business) plus comparison pages, guided by which content AI engines cite. Caveat: the page's own results table shows 22.2% vs 12.2% for 12/01/2024 to 2/15/2025, which does not match the 3.2% baseline or the "one month" claim. — [Profound: Ramp](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/ramp); same 3.2% to 22.2% figure reported by [Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/media/profounds-20m-pitch-deck-brands-show-up-ai-search/)
- **Plaid** (fintech) [V-NAMED]. LLM referral traffic up over 300% "since we onboarded Profound"; conversions from LLM referral traffic up 210%. No dates given. Actions: content optimiser, FAQ generation, 300+ prompt competitive analysis. — [Profound: Plaid](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/plaid)
- **Arizona College of Nursing** (nursing school, 24 local markets) [V-NAMED]. Over 90 days: 51% increase in AI-referred enrollment inquiries, 26% increase in AI-driven website visits, 12% increase in AI visibility, #1 most cited domain on key prompts. Actions: tracked 300+ market-specific prompts across 24 markets, built tuition comparisons and localised FAQs. This is the closest thing to a multi-location local case in Profound's set. — [Profound: AZCN](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/arizona-college-of-nursing)
- **MongoDB** (database software, enterprise) [V-NAMED]. 50% increase in AI search visibility, 5x citations, 90%+ accuracy of LLM answers on MongoDB questions. Actions: benchmarked LLM answers against verified answers, flagged inaccurate citations for the content team "to fix at the source", schema markup updates. No time period given. Directly relevant to Mirror's "fix wrong facts at the source" pitch. — [Profound: MongoDB](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/mongodb)
- **WHOOP** (wearables) [V-NAMED]. 7.9% of AI responses were flagged for accuracy issues (outdated specs, comparisons against old product generations) and routed for correction; 6.6% increase in visibility in 6 months, appearing in 56% of AI answers for top prompts. Actions: detect wrong claims, trace the source, refresh content. — [Profound: WHOOP](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/whoop)
- **Omnilux** (LED skincare, direct to consumer on Shopify) [V-NAMED]. AI-attributed revenue grew from roughly 1% to about 3% of total revenue; LLM-driven traffic up 25% month over month. Actions: PR outreach and content guided by citation data. Period not specified. — [Profound: Omnilux](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/omnilux)
- **Lake.com** (vacation rental startup) [V-NAMED]. 5x branded organic traffic leading into 4 July vs the same period a year earlier; visible in ~50% of lake-house prompts and 30%+ of unbranded prompts. Actions: event, destination and evergreen guide content mapped to AI prompts. — [Profound: Lake.com](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/lake-com)
- **Statsig** (experimentation software) [V-NAMED]. "Nearly doubled our visibility across all answer engines in key categories in less than a week." Actions: comparison content, amplified Reddit threads AI already cited. — [Profound: Statsig](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/statsig)
- **1840 & Company** (remote staffing, mid-market) [V-NAMED]. AI visibility 0% to 6% in two weeks, 11% in one month, from one targeted blog post with segmented headings, short lists, FAQ sections and the brand placed early in a curated ranking. — [Profound: 1840 & Company](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/1840-co-answer-engine-optimization-case-study)
- **Sandler** (sales training) [V-NAMED]. Using HubSpot AEO: 8,000 new website visitors in "just a few weeks", 12 new account conversions (a 10% year-over-year increase), two-point lift in brand visibility. — [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/company-news/hubspot-aeo)
- **Apollo.io** (B2B sales software) [V-NAMED, reported second-hand by HubSpot, no original link]. Built its own subreddit (1,100+ members, 33,400+ views in five months); a comparison thread gained about 3,000 citations across key prompts within a week; brand citation rate 63% on awareness prompts and 36% on category prompts. Tracked in AirOps. — [HubSpot blog, updated 22 Apr 2026](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/answer-engine-optimization-case-studies)

**Vendor-published, customer anonymous**

- **GR0 agency, unnamed health and beauty DTC client** [V-ANON]. LLM-attributed revenue went from about $1,000 to over $100,000 per month "within months". Actions: tracked prompts, listed the URLs LLMs cite most and got earned media and paid affiliate placements on them, wrote 50 to 100 articles per prompt. — [Profound: GR0](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/gr0)
- **Discovered Labs, unnamed European B2B SaaS (100+ employees, under NDA)** [V-ANON]. AI-referred trials rose from 575 to 3,500+ per month in seven weeks. Actions: fixed broken schema and duplicate content, shipped 66 articles in month one, Reddit comments from aged accounts. Measured by self-reported attribution; the page says "600%" and "6x" interchangeably and the URL says "4x". — [Discovered Labs](https://discoveredlabs.com/case-studies/b2b-saas-4x-ai-referred-trials-aeo-strategy)
- **Seer Interactive, one unnamed client** [V-ANON, but an analytics study rather than a sales case]. Published 3 June 2025; GA4 data 1 Oct 2024 to 30 Apr 2025: ChatGPT traffic converted at 15.9%, Perplexity 10.5%, Claude 5%, Gemini 3%, Google organic 1.76%. Only ~11,000 AI sessions vs almost 14 million Google organic sessions (0.07%). — [Seer Interactive](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/case-study-6-learnings-about-how-traffic-from-chatgpt-converts)

**Vendor reporting on itself**

- **Go Fish Digital** [V-SELF]. 24 Sept 2025: +43% monthly AI-referral traffic, +83.33% monthly conversions from AI referrals in under 90 days, AI leads converting at "25X" traditional search. No absolute numbers. Headline says "3X'ing Leads" but 83.33% is about 1.8x. — [Go Fish Digital](https://gofishdigital.com/blog/generative-engine-optimization-geo-case-study/)
- **Broworks** (Webflow agency) [V-SELF]. 9 Aug 2025: after 90 days, 10% of organic traffic came from LLMs and 27% of those sessions became sales-qualified leads. Actions: FAQ / Article / LocalBusiness / Organization schema, FAQ and summary sections, question-style headings. — [Broworks](https://www.broworks.net/blog/answer-engine-optimization-case-study)

**Profound headline-only results (seen on the customer index page; individual story pages not opened)**

- Alchemy "7x higher signup rate from AI-referred traffic"; Aleph "82% growth in LLM-attributed website traffic"; Jordan Digital Marketing "34% revenue growth"; CRS Credit API "20x increase in AI visibility"; OpusClip "45% brand visibility and #1 citation share in 30 days"; Hone "800% visibility boost"; Airbyte "tripled AI brand visibility in one week". — [Profound customers index](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers)

### Inferences
- The most defensible proof for a marketing site is first-party: Vercel (10% of signups), Webflow (10% of signups), Docebo (12.7% of high-intent leads), Tally (#1 referral source), Ahrefs (0.5% of traffic, 12.1% of signups). None are small local businesses.
- Tally is evidence that AI recommends brands with broad genuine third-party presence, not evidence that a GEO service produced the result. It should be cited as "AI referrals can become the top channel", not as "GEO works".
- Vendor case studies mostly report visibility or share-of-voice metrics that the vendor's own tool defines and measures. Only a minority (AZCN, Plaid, Omnilux, GR0, Docebo, Webflow, Vercel) tie to leads, signups or revenue.
- MongoDB and WHOOP are the two public cases closest to Mirror's "find wrong facts and correct them at the source" positioning.

### Gaps
- Could not find or open original case pages for Peec AI, Otterly (the "Instant Commerce 2x" claim appears only in roundups), Scrunch, Goodie, Semrush or Ahrefs customer stories, First Page Sage, NoGood, Omniscient Digital, Siege Media, Exposure Ninja.
- AthenaHQ cases (Grüns share of voice 2.0% to 12.6% in 60 days; Rootly citation rate 3% to 30%; Lago 50% more demos) surfaced only through LinkedIn posts and third-party roundups; no primary athenahq.ai case page was opened. Treat as unverified.
- Profound's Zoom story was named as a starting point but does not appear on the current customer index page.
- No Profound story page shows a publication date.

## 2. Which case studies involve small or local businesses?

### Takeaway
Very few exist, and none found meet a high bar. There is no named, independently verifiable case of a local business (plumber, dentist, law firm, restaurant, salon) with measured results from GEO work. What exists is anonymous agency claims and large studies of how rarely AI recommends local businesses.

### Cited Findings
- **ROI Amplified, unnamed personal injury law firm** [V-ANON]. Post dated 26 Aug 2025: within 90 days, 12% of inbound calls came from ChatGPT-driven leads, with a "20% higher close rate" than Google leads. Actions: conversational FAQ content, directory placements, review workflows, LocalBusiness / LegalService / FAQ schema. No explanation of how call source was tracked. Three other unnamed law-firm anecdotes on the same page give no numbers beyond "cited within 90 days / four months". — [ROI Amplified](https://roiamplified.com/insights/show-up-on-chatgpt/)
- **Arizona College of Nursing** (24 local markets) is the nearest named multi-location case: 51% more AI-referred enrollment inquiries in 90 days using localised FAQs and tuition comparisons. [V-NAMED] — [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/arizona-college-of-nursing)
- **SOCi 2026 Local Visibility Index** (published 28 Jan 2026; 350,000+ locations, 2,751 multi-location brands) [vendor research, not a case study]: ChatGPT recommended 1.2% of brand locations, Gemini 11.0%, Perplexity 7.4% (average 6.5%), versus 35.9% appearing in Google's local 3-pack. Recommended locations average 4.3 stars on ChatGPT, 4.2 on Perplexity, 3.9 on Gemini. Only 45% overlap between top-20 brands in traditional local search and in AI. Top performers manage 100% of Google profiles and 93% of Yelp profiles. — [SOCi press release](http://www.soci.ai/news/in-ai-driven-discovery-few-brands-are-chosen-most-disappear/); slide deck figures: [SOCi webinar PDF](https://golocal.soci.ai/rs/355-TUF-572/images/The_2026_Local_Visibility_Index_SOCi100.pdf?version=0)
- **SOCi on sources**: for local queries LLMs draw on Google Maps (32.5%), brand websites (23.1%), Yelp (10.5%), Facebook (7.6%) and niche sites (26.3%). — [SOCi webinar PDF](https://golocal.soci.ai/rs/355-TUF-572/images/The_2026_Local_Visibility_Index_SOCi100.pdf?version=0)
- **Men's Wearhouse vs Foot Locker** (SOCi example, not small businesses): Men's Wearhouse has 67% presence in the Google 3-pack and a 14% ChatGPT recommendation rate; Foot Locker has 0.0% on both, with only 3% of profiles having social links. — [SOCi webinar PDF](https://golocal.soci.ai/rs/355-TUF-572/images/The_2026_Local_Visibility_Index_SOCi100.pdf?version=0)
- **Local Falcon restaurant study** (3 Mar 2026; 189,905 ChatGPT results, February 2026 data) [vendor research]: 83% of restaurants never appeared in any ChatGPT result for their category and location, versus 14% invisible on Google. Example: Sushikin Grill & Sake Bar, 1,005 Google reviews and 4.8 stars, was invisible in over 50% of ChatGPT queries. Sample is limited to businesses tracked on Local Falcon. — [Local Falcon](https://www.localfalcon.com/research/the-ai-visibility-crisis-why-83-percent-of-restaurants-dont-exist-in-chatgpt)
- **Consumer demand side**: BrightLocal's Local Consumer Review Survey 2026 reports use of ChatGPT and other generative AI tools for local recommendations rose from 6% to 45% in a year, making it the third most popular source. — [BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/) (figure taken from the search result summary of this page; page itself not opened)

### Inferences
- The honest statement for Mirror's site is: "There are almost no published local-business GEO case studies yet." The available local evidence is about the size of the problem (few locations get recommended, facts are often wrong), not about proven fixes.
- The local data that exists supports Mirror's method indirectly: AI pulls local facts from Google Maps, the business website, Yelp and Facebook, so correcting those sources is the logical lever, and a star rating of roughly 4.3+ appears to be a gate for ChatGPT.
- Mirror producing even one named, measured local case study would be differentiated, because competitors do not have one either.

### Gaps
- No case studies on GEO outcomes found from BrightLocal, Whitespark, Sterling Sky, Near Media, Yext or Uberall. Searches for Sterling Sky and Whitespark returned nothing on AI leads.
- "Sommers Schwartz (Detroit law firm): +107% leads from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude after four months" appeared in one search summary attributed to a vendor selling an "AI Monitor"; a follow-up search could not locate the original. Unverified, do not cite.
- Intercore Technologies' widely repeated "Chicago personal injury firm: 156 new clients, $2.34M revenue from AI in six months" (repeated on HubSpot's blog) could NOT be verified: the linked Intercore page, as fetched on 12 Oct 2026, contains no client results at all and says "We frame impact qualitatively on purpose." The figures also do not reconcile (156 clients x $47,500 average is about $7.4M, not $2.34M). Do not cite. — [HubSpot blog](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/answer-engine-optimization-case-studies); [Intercore page](https://intercore.net/llm-seo/case-study-personal-injury-law/)
- No named restaurant, dentist, salon, gym, HVAC or plumber case with numbers was found. Plumber and dentist stories surfaced only as unattributed anecdotes on vendor blogs.

## 3. What independent evidence shows AI referral traffic converts or that AI recommendations drive business?

### Takeaway
The evidence is mixed. Single-site and SaaS data show very high conversion from AI referrals; the larger multi-site and academic studies find AI traffic converts about the same as, or worse than, organic search. Everyone agrees AI referral traffic is growing fast and is still under about 1% of total traffic.

### Cited Findings

**Supports "AI traffic converts better"**
- Ahrefs (single site, 16 June 2025): 0.5% of traffic, 12.1% of signups, 23x conversion vs organic. [1P] — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-traffic-conversions-ahrefs/)
- Seer Interactive (single client, 3 June 2025): ChatGPT 15.9% vs Google organic 1.76%. — [Seer](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/case-study-6-learnings-about-how-traffic-from-chatgpt-converts)
- Microsoft Clarity (6 Nov 2025; 1,277 publisher and news domains, one month): sign-up rate 1.66% from LLMs vs 0.15% search, 0.13% direct, 0.46% social; subscription rate 1.34% vs 0.55% search. AI referrals grew 155.6% over eight months but stayed under 1% of traffic. Sample is publishers only. — [Microsoft Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/blog/ai-traffic-converts-at-3x-the-rate-of-other-channels-study/)
- Semrush (blog dated 21 July 2025): the average AI search visitor is worth 4.4x a traditional organic visitor "based on conversion rate"; projects AI search visitors overtaking traditional search by early 2028. The page gives no sample size or method for the 4.4x figure. — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-search-seo-traffic-study/)
- Adobe Analytics (17 Mar 2025; 1 trillion+ visits to US retail sites): generative AI traffic to US retail up 1,300% in the 2024 holiday season and 1,200% in Feb 2025 vs July 2024; AI visitors show 8% higher engagement, 12% more pages per visit, 23% lower bounce rate, but were 9% LESS likely to convert (the gap was 43% in July 2024). — [Adobe](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2025/03/17/adobe-analytics-traffic-to-us-retail-websites-from-generative-ai-sources-jumps-1200-percent)
- Webflow: ChatGPT traffic converts at 24%, about 6x Google. [1P] — [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/traffic-is-no-longer-reliable)

**Contradicts or tempers it**
- Amsive (3 Sept 2025; 54 sites, six months of GA4): organic 4.60% vs LLM 4.87%, p = 0.794, not statistically significant. LLM traffic was 0.24% of sessions and 0.42% of conversions. Conclusion: no evidence LLM traffic converts better. — [Amsive](https://www.amsive.com/insights/seo/llm-traffic-conversion-rate-study/)
- University of Hamburg / Frankfurt School working paper (Oct 2025; 973 ecommerce sites, Aug 2024 to July 2025, 50,000+ ChatGPT-referred transactions): ChatGPT referrals had lower conversion rates and revenue per session than Google paid and organic search; under 0.2% of sessions. — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/llms-google-referral-conversion-study-463747) (figures from the search result summary; page not opened)
- Visibility Labs (94 ecommerce brands, 2025): ChatGPT 1.81% vs non-branded organic 1.39%, but ChatGPT drove $474,000 vs $32.1 million in revenue and had lower average order value ($204 vs $238). — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/chatgpt-vs-non-branded-organic-search-conversions-470321) (from search result summary; page not opened)

**Scale of the channel**
- Similarweb: AI platforms sent 1.13 billion referral visits in June 2025, up 357% year over year; ChatGPT was over 80% of them. — [Digiday](https://digiday.com/media/in-graphic-detail-the-state-of-ai-referral-traffic-in-2025/) (from search result summary; page not opened)

**Academic: does optimisation change AI visibility?**
- Aggarwal et al., "GEO: Generative Engine Optimization", arXiv Nov 2023, accepted to KDD 2024. On a 10,000-query benchmark, the best methods raised visibility by up to about 40%: adding quotations (~41 to 43%), adding statistics (~33%), citing sources (~28%). Keyword stuffing was about 9 to 10% worse than doing nothing. On live Perplexity (200 queries), quotation addition gave about +22% and statistics addition about +37% on a subjective-impression metric. Lower-ranked sites gained most: citing sources raised visibility 115.1% for the fifth-ranked source and lowered it 30.3% for the top-ranked source. — [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735); [arXiv full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.09735v3)

### Inferences
- A safe, honest summary line: "AI referral traffic is small but growing fast, and several companies report it converts much better than search, though larger studies find it converts about the same."
- The big multipliers (23x, 9x, 6x) come from single SaaS sites or a single client. Multi-site studies show far smaller or no gaps. Quoting 23x without naming Ahrefs as a single-site result would be misleading.
- Last-click analytics probably undercount AI influence, because people see a recommendation in ChatGPT and then search the brand on Google. Docebo and Tally both rely on a "how did you hear about us" question for this reason. Mirror should recommend the same to clients.
- The Princeton-led paper is the only peer-reviewed evidence that content changes move AI visibility, and its finding that smaller, lower-ranked sites gain most is favourable to small businesses. It measured share of words in an answer in a lab setup, not leads or sales.

### Gaps
- Author affiliations for the GEO paper were not shown on the pages fetched (commonly described as Princeton, Georgia Tech, Allen Institute for AI and IIT Delhi; not confirmed here).
- Adobe's later 2025 holiday figures (AI referrals converting 31% better, traffic up 693% to 769%) appeared only in secondary coverage; Adobe's own late-2025 page timed out. Cite only the March 2025 Adobe post unless the newer one is opened.
- No independent study of AI referral conversion specifically for local service businesses was found.

## 4. How often does AI get facts about businesses wrong, and what does that cost?

### Takeaway
The best current numbers: ChatGPT and Perplexity are only about 68% accurate on local business contact information (SOCi, Jan 2026), and 64% of UK high street retailers had at least one false fact returned about them by AI chatbots (Searchable, July 2026). Both come from vendors. One named lawsuit (Wolf River Electric v. Google) puts a dollar figure on the harm.

### Cited Findings
- **SOCi 2026 Local Visibility Index**: ChatGPT, Perplexity and other non-Google LLMs "are only about 68% accurate when providing business contact info"; Gemini, grounded in Google Maps, "has much higher levels of accuracy". — [SOCi webinar PDF, slide 6](https://golocal.soci.ai/rs/355-TUF-572/images/The_2026_Local_Visibility_Index_SOCi100.pdf?version=0)
- **Searchable study of UK high street retailers** (reported 13 July 2026; 72,000+ questions to ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, graded against verified retailer information): 64% of businesses had at least one false fact returned; 1 in 16 answers was false; wrong postcode in about 1 in 10 answers; in 15% of wrong-location cases the address was 20+ miles away; about 1 in 15 website answers pointed to a dead link, lookalike or different business; wrong brand in 1 in 40 answers. Error rate by platform: Perplexity 10%, Gemini 5%, ChatGPT 4%. Smaller businesses were more likely to be affected than larger brands. Searchable sells an AI visibility platform. — [Retail Focus](https://retail-focus.co.uk/nearly-two-thirds-of-uk-high-street-retailers-suffer-from-ai-chatbot-inaccuracies/)
- **Searchable London study**: 93% of 165 London businesses had at least one basic fact wrong or missing across 13,365 questions; about 50% of smaller businesses got at least one false fact vs 32% of larger companies. — reported in [Search Engine Journal, 20 July 2026](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/ai-answers-about-your-locations-are-often-wrong-check-before-customers-do/582205/)
- **WHOOP**: 7.9% of AI responses about the brand were flagged for accuracy issues. [V-NAMED] — [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/whoop)
- **BrightLocal** (6 July 2023, old and tiny: five pizza queries in Brighton, UK): inaccuracies in 80% of Bard results, 60% of ChatGPT, 20% of Bing Chat, 0% for Google and Bing search. Denominator not defined. — [BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/generative-ai-in-local-search-case-study/)
- **Named incident, Wolf River Electric v. Google** (Minnesota solar installer; complaint filed March 2025): Google's AI Overview allegedly stated the company was "currently facing a lawsuit from the Minnesota Attorney General", which the company says is false. It says several customers ended relationships in March 2025, with contracts worth as much as $150,000. Damages sought "in excess of $50,000" per count per this report. Claims are allegations; no ruling on the merits was found. — [Minnesota Lawyer, 13 June 2025](https://minnlawyer.com/2025/06/13/wolf-river-electric-google-ai-defamation-lawsuit/)
- **Fixing accuracy is linked to visibility gains in vendor cases**: MongoDB reports 90%+ LLM answer accuracy after a loop that flags inaccurate citations and fixes content at the source, alongside +50% visibility. — [Profound: MongoDB](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/mongodb)

### Inferences
- "About one in three" (SOCi's 68% accurate) and "64% of retailers had at least one false fact" (Searchable) are the two most quotable accuracy stats, and both must be attributed to the vendor that produced them.
- The Searchable finding that small businesses are hit harder, because they have fewer accurate third-party sources, is the single most on-message finding for Mirror.
- Per-answer error rates are modest (4 to 10%), but because a business has many facts, most businesses end up with at least one wrong. Both framings are true; the per-business framing is the more striking and should be stated precisely.
- There is no study that quantifies lost revenue from wrong AI facts. Wolf River is the only dollar-figure example and it is an unproven allegation about reputation, not about wrong hours or prices.

### Gaps
- Other reports of Wolf River's claimed damages ($110 million to $210 million) and of the case being remanded to state court in January 2026 appeared only in search summaries and were not verified at source.
- No study measuring AI error rates specifically for opening hours or prices of US small businesses was found. BrightLocal has no current accuracy study; Yext and Uberall accuracy studies were not found.
- A reported incident of Google AI inventing specials for a Missouri pizzeria could not be located by search; not included.
- No Profound-wide "analysis of AI claims" study was found beyond the WHOOP and MongoDB stories.

## 5. Which specific tactics do the case studies credit, and how often does each recur?

### Takeaway
Across the ~20 verified cases, the most repeated tactics are answer-style and comparison content (nearly all), FAQ sections, structured data, and Reddit or community presence. Listings and review work appears mainly in local research rather than in case studies. Fixing wrong facts at the source appears in only two cases (MongoDB, WHOOP).

### Cited Findings
Rough tally from the cases opened (out of about 20):
- **New or rewritten content aimed at the questions people ask AI, including comparison pages** (about 14): Ramp, Plaid, AZCN, Lake.com, Statsig, 1840 & Company, GR0, Discovered Labs, Go Fish Digital, Broworks, Vercel, Docebo, ROI Amplified law firm, Apollo. — e.g. [Ramp](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/ramp), [Go Fish Digital](https://gofishdigital.com/blog/generative-engine-optimization-geo-case-study/)
- **FAQ sections** (about 6): Plaid, AZCN, 1840 & Company, Broworks, ROI Amplified law firm, Intercore-style legal playbooks. — [1840 & Company](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/1840-co-answer-engine-optimization-case-study), [AZCN](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/arizona-college-of-nursing)
- **Structured data / schema markup** (about 6): MongoDB, Vercel, Broworks, Discovered Labs (fixing broken schema), Go Fish Digital, ROI Amplified law firm. — [Vercel](https://vercel.com/blog/how-were-adapting-seo-for-llms-and-ai-search), [Broworks](https://www.broworks.net/blog/answer-engine-optimization-case-study)
- **Reddit and community presence** (about 7): Statsig, GR0 (Reddit citation share up to 10%), Discovered Labs, Apollo (own subreddit), Webflow, Vercel, Tally. — [Statsig](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/statsig), [Webflow](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/traffic-is-no-longer-reliable), [Tally](https://blog.tally.so/from-2-to-3m-arr-how-we-bootstrapped-tally-with-a-tiny-team/)
- **Third-party mentions, earned media, PR, "best of" placements** (about 4): GR0 (earned media and paid affiliate placements on the URLs LLMs cite), Omnilux (PR outreach), 1840 & Company (brand placed early in curated rankings), Tally (organic mentions). — [GR0](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/gr0), [Omnilux](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/omnilux)
- **Finding and correcting inaccurate AI answers at the source** (2): MongoDB, WHOOP. — [MongoDB](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/mongodb), [WHOOP](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/whoop)
- **Listings, directories and reviews** (1 case, plus local research): ROI Amplified law firm (directory placements, review workflows); SOCi finds top AI performers manage 100% of Google profiles and 93% of Yelp profiles and average 4.5 stars on Google. — [ROI Amplified](https://roiamplified.com/insights/show-up-on-chatgpt/), [SOCi PDF](https://golocal.soci.ai/rs/355-TUF-572/images/The_2026_Local_Visibility_Index_SOCi100.pdf?version=0)
- **Adding statistics, quotations and cited sources to content**: backed by the academic GEO paper (+28% to +43%) and used by Go Fish Digital ("fact density") and Vercel. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.09735v3)
- **Tactics that did not work**: keyword stuffing (about 9 to 10% worse than baseline in the GEO paper); llms.txt (Webflow tested it and saw no significant lift). — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.09735v3), [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/traffic-is-no-longer-reliable)
- **Yext citation-source study** (press release, 9 Oct 2025; 6.8 million citations across 1.6 million AI responses): 86% of AI citations came from sources brands control: first-party websites 44%, listings 42%, reviews and social 8%, forums such as Reddit about 2% for location-based queries. — [Yext press release via Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251009106549/en/Yext-Research-86-of-AI-Citations-Come-from-Brand-Managed-Sources-Clarifying-How-Marketers-Can-Compete-in-the-AI-Search-Era) (page returned HTTP 403; figures are from the search result summary of the release)

### Inferences
- There is a split: SaaS case studies lean on content and Reddit; local evidence (SOCi, Yext) says listings, the business's own website and reviews are what AI actually reads for local questions. Reddit matters far less for local queries (about 2% per Yext). Mirror's listings-and-facts approach matches the local evidence better than the SaaS playbook does.
- Schema markup is frequently credited but never isolated; no case shows a lift from schema alone.
- Content volume tactics (66 articles in a month, 50 to 100 articles per prompt) are not realistic for a small business, and Reddit comments from "aged accounts" is a tactic Mirror should not copy.

### Gaps
- No case isolates the effect of a single tactic; all report bundles.
- No case study measures the effect of fixing listings or citations on AI recommendations for a local business.
- Yext's figures could not be read at the primary source.

## 6. How long did results take in each case?

### Takeaway
Visibility metrics moved in one week to one month in vendor cases; lead, signup and revenue changes were reported over about 90 days to 6 months. A fair claim is "visibility changes within weeks, business results in about three months".

### Cited Findings
- Under 1 week: Statsig (visibility nearly doubled) — [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/statsig); Apollo (about 3,000 citations within a week of a comparison thread) — [HubSpot blog](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/answer-engine-optimization-case-studies)
- 2 weeks to 1 month: 1840 & Company (0% to 6% in two weeks, 11% in a month) — [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/1840-co-answer-engine-optimization-case-study); Ramp (3.2% to 22.2% "within a month") — [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/ramp)
- 7 weeks: Discovered Labs client (575 to 3,500+ AI-referred trials) — [Discovered Labs](https://discoveredlabs.com/case-studies/b2b-saas-4x-ai-referred-trials-aeo-strategy)
- About 90 days: Arizona College of Nursing (+51% inquiries) — [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/arizona-college-of-nursing); Go Fish Digital — [Go Fish Digital](https://gofishdigital.com/blog/generative-engine-optimization-geo-case-study/); Broworks — [Broworks](https://www.broworks.net/blog/answer-engine-optimization-case-study); ROI Amplified law firm — [ROI Amplified](https://roiamplified.com/insights/show-up-on-chatgpt/)
- About 6 months: Vercel (1% to 10% of signups) — [Vercel](https://vercel.com/blog/how-were-adapting-seo-for-llms-and-ai-search); WHOOP (+6.6% visibility) — [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/whoop)
- About 12 months: Docebo (AI share of high-intent leads up 429% year over year) — [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/ai-discovery-playbook); Webflow (4x year over year) — [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com/p/traffic-is-no-longer-reliable)
- "Within months", no period given: GR0 client, Plaid, MongoDB, Omnilux — [GR0](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/gr0), [Plaid](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/plaid)

### Inferences
- The fastest results come from companies that already had strong authority (Ramp, Statsig, Go Fish Digital, which says so itself). A new or small local business should expect the slower end.
- Some of the growth in every 2025 case coincides with ChatGPT's own user growth and its April 2025 browsing change, so not all of it is attributable to the work done.

### Gaps
- No timeline data for local businesses beyond the anonymous law-firm claim of 90 days.
- No case reports whether gains persisted after the first measurement window.

## 7. Which commonly repeated GEO statistics are weak, unsourced or contested?

### Takeaway
Several of the most-quoted numbers are single-site results, vendor figures with no method, or cannot be found at their supposed source. The conversion multipliers and the Intercore law-firm case are the ones most likely to embarrass anyone who repeats them.

### Cited Findings
- **"AI visitors convert 23x better"**: real, but one site (Ahrefs), one 30-day window, and the author says it probably will not scale. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-traffic-conversions-ahrefs/)
- **"AI search visitors are worth 4.4x"** (Semrush): the page gives no sample size, data source or calculation for the figure; projections are extrapolations. Semrush sells AI visibility tools. — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/ai-search-seo-traffic-study/)
- **"ChatGPT converts at 15.9% vs 1.76%"** (Seer): one unnamed client, ~11,000 AI sessions; the same post's "$205,000 pipeline" is illustrative (1,370 conversions x an assumed $150). — [Seer](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/case-study-6-learnings-about-how-traffic-from-chatgpt-converts)
- **Directly contested**: Amsive's 54-site study found no statistically significant difference (4.87% vs 4.60%, p = 0.794). — [Amsive](https://www.amsive.com/insights/seo/llm-traffic-conversion-rate-study/); the Hamburg / Frankfurt 973-site ecommerce paper found ChatGPT referrals converting worse than Google. — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/llms-google-referral-conversion-study-463747)
- **"AI traffic converts at 3x"** (Microsoft Clarity headline): the "3x" is not defined in the article; the sample is publisher and news sites only; conversion is sign-up or subscription. — [Microsoft Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/blog/ai-traffic-converts-at-3x-the-rate-of-other-channels-study/)
- **"GEO boosts visibility by 40%"**: a lab result on a benchmark using GPT-3.5 and a word-share metric, for the best of several methods; it is not a traffic or revenue lift, and top-ranked sources actually lost visibility (-30.3%) under one method. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.09735v3)
- **Ramp "7x in 30 days"**: Profound's own results table shows 22.2% vs 12.2% over 12/01/2024 to 2/15/2025, which conflicts with the 3.2% baseline and the one-month claim in the text. — [Profound: Ramp](https://www.tryprofound.com/customers/ramp)
- **Go Fish Digital "3X'ing Leads"**: the reported lift is +83.33% (about 1.8x), no absolute numbers, and the "25X conversion rate" has no stated method. — [Go Fish Digital](https://gofishdigital.com/blog/generative-engine-optimization-geo-case-study/)
- **Intercore "$2.34M from AI for a Chicago PI firm"**: not present on the linked source page as of 12 Oct 2026, and internally inconsistent. Unverifiable. — [HubSpot blog](https://blog.hubspot.com/marketing/answer-engine-optimization-case-studies); [Intercore](https://intercore.net/llm-seo/case-study-personal-injury-law/)
- **Discovered Labs "6x / 600% / 4x"**: the same case is described three ways; the baseline and result are self-reported attribution with no confirmation they were measured the same way. — [Discovered Labs](https://discoveredlabs.com/case-studies/b2b-saas-4x-ai-referred-trials-aeo-strategy)
- **Vercel "10% of signups"**: genuine first-party figure, but dates conflict. Vercel's June 2025 blog says 4.8% "the previous month" while the CEO's 4.8% post predates his 10% post by about a month in spring 2025; many roundups date it wrongly (one says October 2025). Quote it as "Vercel reported in 2025". — [Vercel blog](https://vercel.com/blog/how-were-adapting-seo-for-llms-and-ai-search); [X post](https://x.com/rauchg/status/1910093634445422639)
- **Tally "grew $2M to $3M ARR thanks to ChatGPT"**: Tally's post says ChatGPT became the #1 referral source and credits organic community presence; it does not say GEO work caused the ARR growth. Roundups overstate it. — [Tally](https://blog.tally.so/from-2-to-3m-arr-how-we-bootstrapped-tally-with-a-tiny-team/)
- **Gartner "search volume will drop 25% by 2026"**: a February 2024 prediction with no published method, criticised at the time. — [Search Engine Journal](https://searchenginejournal.com/why-prediction-of-25-search-volume-drop-due-to-chatbots-fails-scrutiny/511270) (from search result summary; page not opened)
- **SOCi "1.2% of locations recommended by ChatGPT" and "30x harder"**: real figure, but it covers multi-location brand locations only, counts a location as recommended only if it appears in positions 1 to 5 of a "recommend 10 businesses" prompt, and the 30x compares ChatGPT's 1.2% with Google's 35.9%; the average across the three AI platforms is 6.5%. — [SOCi PDF](https://golocal.soci.ai/rs/355-TUF-572/images/The_2026_Local_Visibility_Index_SOCi100.pdf?version=0)
- **Local Falcon "83% of restaurants don't exist in ChatGPT"**: restaurants only, only businesses tracked by Local Falcon, one month of data. — [Local Falcon](https://www.localfalcon.com/research/the-ai-visibility-crisis-why-83-percent-of-restaurants-dont-exist-in-chatgpt)
- **Searchable "64% of retailers have a false fact"**: vendor study; the report does not state how many retailers were tested. — [Retail Focus](https://retail-focus.co.uk/nearly-two-thirds-of-uk-high-street-retailers-suffer-from-ai-chatbot-inaccuracies/)
- **BrightLocal "45% use AI for local recommendations"**: it covers all generative AI tools, not ChatGPT alone, and is a survey of about 1,000 US adults; some blogs misstate it as "45% of local discovery". — [BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/) (from search result summary)

### Inferences
- Rule of thumb for Mirror's site: quote a number only with the company name, the year and the word "reported" (for example "Vercel reported in 2025 that ChatGPT referred about 10% of new signups"), and never present a single-company conversion multiplier as a general fact.
- Visibility-percentage gains are measured by the vendor's own tool on a prompt set the vendor or customer chose, so they are not comparable across vendors and are easy to inflate.
- The safest claims are the ones several unrelated sources agree on: AI referral traffic is growing quickly, is still roughly 1% or less of traffic, AI assistants recommend few local businesses, and they get basic business facts wrong often enough to matter.

### Gaps
- No independent audit of any vendor case study was found.
- Could not check whether Gartner's 25% prediction came true; no 2026 data found.
- Several Search Engine Land pages returned HTTP 403, so the Hamburg / Frankfurt paper, Visibility Labs and the SEL write-up of SOCi (which reportedly says Gemini was "100% accurate") were not read directly.
