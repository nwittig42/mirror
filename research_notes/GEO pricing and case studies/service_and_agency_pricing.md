# Done-for-you GEO / AEO / AI visibility service and agency pricing (US, October 2026)

All pages accessed 2026-10-12. Reading notes for the report writer:

- Almost every source below is an agency or tool vendor with a commercial interest. Independent survey data on GEO pricing is close to non-existent; the one large dataset (First Page Sage, Oct 2026) is published by an agency that sells GEO.
- "Verified on page" means I fetched the page and read the figures. "Search snippet only" means the figure came from a search result summary and the page itself was not opened; treat those as weaker.
- Mirror's draft prices for reference: $495 / $995 / $1,995 per month, month to month, free first scan.

## 1. What do named agencies and productized services charge for GEO / AEO / AI visibility retainers in 2026?

### Takeaway
Published "small business" GEO retainers from mainstream agencies start around $1,500 to $3,000 per month (First Page Sage's Starter-tier median is $2,950), while a thin layer of productized local-focused sellers sits at $499 to $1,997 per month. Mirror's $495 / $995 / $1,995 ladder sits below the agency floor and inside the productized local band.

### Cited Findings

**Largest dataset (vendor-published, single source)**
- First Page Sage "GEO & AEO Pricing Statistics: 2026 Report", published October 7, 2026: 1,146 price quotes from 214 agencies collected July 6 to October 2, 2026 (published pricing pages, client proposals, signed contracts). — [First Page Sage](https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/geo-aeo-pricing-statistics/)
- Tiers in that report: Starter (local and small businesses) $1,800–$4,200/mo, median $2,950, 3-month minimum, 3.4 deliverables/mo; Growth (mid-market) $4,500–$11,000, median $7,400, 6-month minimum; Advanced $11,000–$22,000, median $14,600, 6-month minimum; Enterprise $22,000–$60,000+, median $31,500, 12-month minimum. — [First Page Sage](https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/geo-aeo-pricing-statistics/)

### Inferences
- There are two distinct markets. Agencies selling content-heavy GEO to small businesses cluster at $1,500–$4,200/mo with 3 to 6 month minimums. Productized local sellers cluster at $499–$1,997/mo and tend toward month to month. Mirror's ladder matches the second group almost tier for tier ($495 vs ZeroGravity $499 and AI Search Rankings $799; $995 vs $1,500; $1,995 vs AudienceIntent $1,997).
- Mirror's top tier ($1,995) is below the First Page Sage Starter median ($2,950) and below WebFX's lowest plan ($3,000), so "less than an agency's cheapest plan" is a defensible claim at every tier.
- Home services has the lowest industry median ($3,190) and legal one of the highest ($8,170). A single price ladder for plumbers and law firms likely leaves money on the table with law firms; vertical pricing or a higher tier for legal is worth testing.
- Demand Local's delivery-cost estimate ($800–$1,500/mo for a basic local tier) implies that a $495 tier only works if delivery is heavily automated.

### Gaps

## 2. What do one-off GEO / AI visibility audits cost, and how many sellers give the first audit away free?

### Takeaway
Paid agency audits run about $1,500–$5,000 (median $4,300 in the First Page Sage data), marketplace gigs run $20 to a few hundred dollars, and a free first audit is the standard lead magnet among small-business-facing sellers. A free first scan is table stakes, not a differentiator.

### Cited Findings
- One-time audits are 8% of GEO/AEO engagements; range $1,500–$12,000, median $4,300, 2–6 weeks. — [First Page Sage](https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/geo-aeo-pricing-statistics/)
- "Tripwire audit" priced $1,500–$5,000 one-time, with a target audit-to-retainer conversion of 40–60% (agency guidance, not measured data). — [Demand Local](https://www.demandlocal.com/blog/how-to-price-geo-services/)
- Project-based consulting (one-time audit, scoping or second opinion) $5,000–$50,000 per project. — [Nutshell](https://www.nutshell.com/blog/geo-costs)
- Freelance / project work $150–$2,000 one-time. — [AudienceIntent](https://www.audienceintent.ai/insights/how-much-does-ai-search-visibility-cost/)
- Agency sales guides suggest audits at $500–$2,000 as a paid entry point (search snippet only). — [Passionfruit](https://www.getpassionfruit.com/blog/how-to-sell-geo-services-to-your-seo-clients-the-agency-playbook)
- Fiverr (search snippets only, not verified on the listings): a full GEO audit plus action plan gig at $20 with 4-day delivery; other GEO gigs listed at $100–$200 and $200–$400. — [Fiverr gig](https://fiverr.com/sachin_binder/do-generative-engine-optimization); [Fiverr monthly GEO gig](https://fiverr.com/hafiz_gm/do-generative-engine-optimization-monthly-geo-service)
- Self-serve audit tools: Pixelmojo Radar $0 technical readiness check, $5 single full audit, $12 and $40 packs. — [Pixelmojo](https://www.pixelmojo.io/answers/how-much-does-an-ai-visibility-audit-cost)
- Free audits confirmed on page: HireAWiz ("A $349 value, yours free. Five per week"; delivered live on Zoom in two to three business days; covers ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, competitor benchmark, nine-point technical scorecard). — [HireAWiz](https://www.hireawiz.com/aeo-audit/)
- AI Search Rankings: free AI visibility audit tool, free website audit, free page audit, "no obligation to proceed". — [AI Search Rankings](https://www.aisearchrankings.com/pricing.php)
- AudienceIntent: free AI visibility audit, no signup. — [AudienceIntent](https://www.audienceintent.ai/insights/how-much-does-ai-search-visibility-cost/)
- DerivateX: free cross-LLM brand accuracy audit as the main call to action. — [DerivateX](https://derivatex.agency/use-cases/fix-ai-hallucinations-about-your-brand/)
- Invisio (Poland, not US): free audit of 8–10 customer questions asked to ChatGPT three times each, PDF in 3 business days; notes that among Polish agencies the audit is "free at some agencies, a few hundred PLN at others, sometimes credited toward a subscription". — [Invisio](https://invisio.digital/en/blog/ai-search-optimization-cost/)
- ZeroGravity takes the opposite approach: a $199 one-time setup fee that covers the initial audit. — [ZeroGravity](https://zegravity.com/blog/geo-agency-pricing-guide-2026)

### Inferences
- Of the six small-business-facing sellers whose pages I opened, five offer a free first audit and one charges $199 for it. I could not find a count across the wider market.
- HireAWiz's "$349 value" is the only stated standalone price for a small-business audit; it is a useful anchor for putting a dollar value on Mirror's free scan.
- Capping free audits ("five per week") is used to add scarcity.

### Gaps
- No market-wide statistic on the share of GEO sellers offering a free audit.
- Upwork: no listings surfaced. Fiverr prices not verified on the live listings.

- Listings software at $15–$65/mo already claims to keep facts consistent across directories, and BrightLocal now includes AI visibility tracking at $41/mo. Owners comparing on price will see these as the cheap alternative; Mirror's difference has to be the done-for-you fixing and the fact-check against an approved fact sheet, not the monitoring.
- The reputation platforms mostly require annual contracts, so month to month is a real advantage against that reference point.

## 4. How are GEO services usually packaged?

### Takeaway
The flat monthly retainer dominates (68% of engagements in the First Page Sage data), usually in three tiers scaled by number of prompts tracked, competitors monitored and pages or content pieces delivered. Per-location and per-prompt pricing as the headline unit is rare, and performance-based pricing is about 5%.

### Cited Findings
- Model shares: custom monthly retainer 41% (median $7,200/mo); tiered package retainer 27% (median $6,300/mo); project or sprint 13% (median $14,500/project); one-time audit 8%; hourly consulting 6% ($125–$325/hr, median $205); performance-based 5% ($1,500–$4,000 base plus per-result fees, median base $2,600). — [First Page Sage](https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/geo-aeo-pricing-statistics/)
- Buyers of performance-based deals "reported difficulty agreeing on how results are counted". — [First Page Sage](https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/geo-aeo-pricing-statistics/)
- Four agency models: audit ($1,500–$5,000 one-time), retainer ($3,000–$10,000+), hybrid SEO+GEO add-on (+20–30% on existing SEO fees), white-label resale (40–60% markup on wholesale). — [Demand Local](https://www.demandlocal.com/blog/how-to-price-geo-services/)
- Flat add-on framing: bolt AI visibility monitoring onto an existing SEO retainer for $500–$1,000/mo (search snippet only). — [LLM Pulse](https://llmpulse.ai/blog/geo-agency-guide/)
- Generalist agencies adding GEO to SEO: $500–$1,500/mo (search snippet only). — [Over The Top SEO](https://www.overthetopseo.com/geo-pricing-guide-how-to-price-ai-search-optimization-services/)
- Tier scaling by prompts: WebFX scales every tier by prompts monitored (50, 100, 150, 250, 500, 1,000, 2,000) and "flexible assets" per month (3 to 90). — [WebFX](https://www.webfx.com/seo/pricing/ai-seo/)
- Tier scaling for local: AI Search Rankings scales by local prompts tracked (25 / 50–75 / 150–250), competitors (3 / 5 / 10) and pages optimized (1 / 2–3 / 5–8); multi-location is pushed to the top tier rather than priced per location. — [AI Search Rankings](https://www.aisearchrankings.com/pricing.php)
- Fixed 30-day campaigns as an alternative: Digital Elevator $3,495–$22,995 per campaign. — [First Page Sage](https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/geo-aeo-pricing-statistics/)
- Per-location pricing is the norm in the adjacent listings and reputation categories (BrightLocal, Moz Local, Birdeye). — [BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/pricing/); [HiFiveStar](https://hifivestar.com/blog/reputation-management-cost/)
- Nutshell's guide says businesses with five or more locations should expect retainers to reflect that complexity but gives no per-location figure. — [Nutshell](https://www.nutshell.com/blog/geo-costs)

### Inferences
- For small businesses the most common package is a three-tier flat monthly retainer, with a free audit in front and a "most popular" middle tier. Mirror's structure matches the norm.
- Competitors differentiate tiers by volume (prompts, pages, competitors). None of the small-business sellers I found tier by accuracy work (number of facts checked, number of sources fixed). That is open space for Mirror's tier logic.
- Mirror will need a stated multi-location rule; the market either pushes multi-location to the top tier or quotes custom.

### Gaps
- No source prices GEO per location or per prompt as the headline unit, so I cannot say what such pricing would be.
- No data on which packaging converts best for small businesses.

## 5. What contract terms and setup fees are standard?

### Takeaway
Agencies typically require 3 months at the small-business tier and 6 months at mid-market; productized local sellers are more often month to month with no setup fee. Month to month with no setup fee is competitive but not unique.

### Cited Findings
- Minimum terms by tier: Starter 3 months, Growth 6, Advanced 6, Enterprise 12. Typical term for custom retainers 6 months; tiered packages 3–6 months. — [First Page Sage](https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/geo-aeo-pricing-statistics/)
- WebFX: six-month minimum on all plans; no setup fee listed. — [WebFX](https://www.webfx.com/seo/pricing/ai-seo/)
- First Page Sage's own service: month to month. DerivateX: 90-day sprint. Digital Elevator: 30 days. — [First Page Sage](https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/geo-aeo-pricing-statistics/)
- Entry-level retainers "often 6 to 12 months"; typical agency setup fees $2,500–$12,000+ (the author sells a no-setup-fee alternative, so this framing is self-serving). — [AudienceIntent](https://www.audienceintent.ai/insights/how-much-does-ai-search-visibility-cost/)

## 6. Is there evidence of what small businesses would pay for AI visibility, or of price resistance?

### Takeaway
I found no direct willingness-to-pay data for AI visibility among small businesses. Indirect evidence points to low urgency (about half of owners think AI search will not affect them) and a ceiling well under agency rates, with tools at $20–$99/mo as the price anchor most owners see.

### Cited Findings
- Digital Third Coast survey of 803 small business owners, October 2025 (older than 2026): 42% have a dedicated SEO budget; 13% cut SEO spend in 2025, by an average of 31%; 1 in 3 are anxious about how AI search will affect them; 39% think AI search will benefit their business, 48% think it will have no effect, 13% think it will hurt; 8% say AI search matters most, 50% say a mix of Google and LLM visibility is ideal. The survey notes marketing is "typically a DIY affair" for these owners. It did not ask what they would pay. — [Digital Third Coast](https://www.digitalthirdcoast.com/blog/seo-llm-business-owner-survey)
- Squarespace: nearly 6 in 10 small businesses say they do not know where to start with AI search optimization (search snippet only). — [Squarespace](https://www.squarespace.com/blog/ai-search-for-business-owners)
- Global Payments survey of 1,000 US SMBs: 72% believe customer reviews are key to AI visibility (search snippet only, syndicated). — [Alabama Gazette / Stacker](https://www.alabamagazette.com/story/2026/05/01/news/72-percent-of-small-businesses-say-this-is-the-key-to-ai-visibility/11045.html)
- Bluevine, 942 owners, April 2026: cost is a barrier to deeper AI use for 24% (general AI, not AI visibility; search snippet only). — [Bluevine](https://www.bluevine.com/blog/small-business-ai-trends-report-2026)
- Most small-business AI visibility trackers cost $20–$99/mo (search snippet only). — [Lighthouse Local](https://www.lighthouselocal.ai/blog/ai-search-visibility-tools-for-small-businesses)
- Agency-side view of the ceiling: small businesses "cannot support $5,000+/month retainers". — [Demand Local](https://www.demandlocal.com/blog/how-to-price-geo-services/)
- One reputation-cost guide suggests most small businesses should start with a one-shot report rather than a retainer (search snippet only). — [Stork.ai](https://www.stork.ai/blog/ai-reputation-management-cost)
- Agency playbooks recommend a low-priced fixed-fee 90-day pilot or a paid audit as the entry offer to get past price hesitation (search snippet only). — [Passionfruit](https://www.getpassionfruit.com/blog/how-to-sell-geo-services-to-your-seo-clients-the-agency-playbook)

### Inferences
- Demand is real but soft: about a third of owners are worried, about half see no effect. The buyers most likely to pay are the worried third, and a concrete "here is what AI says wrong about you" scan is the natural way to move the indifferent half.
- With 58% of owners having no dedicated SEO budget and DIY as the default, $495/mo is a meaningful ask for a bakery or gym; it is a small one for a law firm or dentist where one new client covers months of fees.
- The repeated advice to lead with an audit or short pilot suggests owners resist committing to a retainer before seeing proof.

### Gaps
- No Reddit or agency-forum threads were retrievable (the search tool returned no Reddit results on two attempts), so there is no anecdotal closed-deal pricing from owners or agency owners in these notes.
- No survey asks small business owners what they would pay per month for AI visibility or for AI accuracy.
- No churn or retention data for small-business GEO retainers.

## 7. Do any providers sell accuracy / wrong-fact correction as the headline, and at what price?

### Takeaway
Accuracy is mostly sold as a feature or a blog topic, not a product. The one provider I found leading with "fix what AI gets wrong" (DerivateX) targets B2B SaaS at $5,000–$12,000/mo; nobody found sells it as the headline to local small businesses at a published price.

### Cited Findings
- DerivateX has a page titled "ChatGPT Describing Your Product Wrong? Fix AI Hallucinations": audit across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude, mapping of sources feeding wrong information, schema, an AI information page and llms.txt, cross-platform consistency audit, outreach to correct third-party sources. Claims improved descriptions in 2 to 6 weeks and full correction in 6 to 12 weeks. No price on the page; entry is a free brand accuracy audit. Targets B2B SaaS at $5M–$50M ARR. — [DerivateX](https://derivatex.agency/use-cases/fix-ai-hallucinations-about-your-brand/)
- DerivateX's general retainers are $5,000–$12,000/mo on a 90-day sprint. — [First Page Sage](https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/geo-aeo-pricing-statistics/)
- AI Search Rankings includes "AI answer accuracy monitoring" only in its top $3,500/mo local tier, and lists an unpriced "AI Liability Assessment". — [AI Search Rankings](https://www.aisearchrankings.com/pricing.php)
- Pixelmojo Radar sells an "LLM-powered citation and hallucination layer" inside a $5 audit or $199/mo self-serve plan (detection only, no fixing). — [Pixelmojo](https://www.pixelmojo.io/answers/how-much-does-an-ai-visibility-audit-cost)
- Yext Scout is described by one reviewer as tracking where a business is mentioned or summarized inaccurately; it is a paid add-on with no public pricing, sold with Yext's enterprise offer (search snippet only). — [Trakkr](https://trakkr.ai/alternatives/yextscout-alternatives)
- Several vendors publish how-to content on wrong AI answers (fix the sources, not the model; vendor estimate of 60–180 days to correct) without a priced service attached (search snippets only). — [Texta](https://www.texta.ai/blog/when-chatgpt-gets-brand-wrong-respond-guide); [Growtika](https://growtika.com/geo-hub/chatgpt-wrong-info)

### Inferences
- "We fix what AI gets wrong about your business" as the lead promise for local small businesses appears unclaimed at a published price. The closest local competitor puts accuracy monitoring behind a $3,500 tier, which makes Mirror's inclusion of it at $495 a strong contrast.
- Because there is no accuracy-priced comparable, Mirror's prices can only be benchmarked against visibility retainers and adjacent services, not against a like-for-like accuracy service.
- Published correction timelines (2–12 weeks from one vendor, 60–180 days from another) suggest a month-to-month customer may want to cancel before fixes show up in AI answers; the weekly scan and monthly report need to show progress at the source level early.

### Gaps
- Search was limited; there may be small local providers with accuracy-first offers that did not surface. This is "none found", not "none exist".
- No price for Yext Scout, and no evidence that it corrects errors rather than recommends fixes.
- No data on whether accuracy framing converts better or worse than visibility framing with small business owners.
