# Off-site tactics that raise AI answer-engine mentions (2025-2026)

Evidence grades used below: **A** = large dataset or primary/official source; **B** = vendor study with a stated method (the vendor may have a commercial interest); **C** = agency or vendor opinion, self-reported, or second-hand; **D** = no evidence found. Almost every number is correlational. No controlled causal study was found for any tactic.

## "Best of" / listicle placement

### Takeaway
Listicles are the single biggest cited page type in ChatGPT for "best X" questions (43.8% of source pages in Ahrefs' study, grade A/B). Where a brand sits in the list matters, and most cited lists were recently updated. For local businesses, expert surveys rank presence on curated "best of" lists as the #1 AI-visibility factor. No good evidence was found on pay-to-play lists. This is the highest-value tactic to drive straight from audit data: the cited list URLs are the outreach list.

### Cited Findings
- Ahrefs (Glen Allsopp, Dec 4 2025) studied ChatGPT answers to 750 top-of-funnel prompts across software, products and agencies, covering about 10k third-party URLs. "Best X" blog lists made up 43.8% of all source page types. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/best-lists-research/)
- In a hand review of 750 cited lists, brands ranked higher on a list showed up more often in ChatGPT answers. The effect was weaker in lists of 10+ items, and the author warns about list-length bias. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/best-lists-research/)
- Of 1,100 cited lists, 79.1% were last updated in 2025 and 26% in the past two months; 57.1% had been updated since first publication. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/best-lists-research/)
- 35% of cited "best" lists were on low-authority domains (by Ahrefs DR), so small blogs count. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/best-lists-research/)
- When a software brand was recommended, its own self-published list appeared in more than a third of those ChatGPT answers. In Google, 67.6% of 250 software "best" searches included a list where the publisher ranked itself first. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/best-lists-research/)
- Ahrefs' own advice: keep your product and service pages current, aim for high positions on third-party lists, and publicize awards and analyst recognition. If you publish your own lists, keep them rare, don't always rank yourself first, and link to competitors. The study does not address paid placement. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/best-lists-research/)
- Being cited is not the same as being recommended: in an Ahrefs experiment, 43% of answers that cited one of the self-promotional pages never mentioned the promoted product. — [Ahrefs experiment](https://ahrefs.com/blog/self-promotional-content-ai-seo-experiment/)
- Ahrefs also published a list of the "50 best PR pitching opportunities in ChatGPT", meaning cited pages that agencies can pitch. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/pr-pitching-opportunities-chatgpt/)
- **Local:** Whitespark's 2026 Local Search Ranking Factors (47 experts scoring 187 factors, published Nov 2025) added an "AI search visibility" score. The #1 AI factor was "Presence of business on expert-curated best-of and similar lists" (179). #3 was "prominence on key industry-relevant domains" (167). — [Whitespark](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)
- Secondary write-ups give the listicle share as about 40% or 43.8%, and one comes from a company that sells listicle placements, so it has an incentive. — [The HOTH](https://thehoth.com/blog/ai-cites-listicles/); [Quattr](https://www.quattr.com/blog/takeaway-from-ahrefs-ai-search-study)

### Inferences
- An agency workflow: (1) pull every cited URL from audit answers where the client was absent; (2) classify each as a listicle, review site, forum, video, directory or news; (3) for listicles, find the author or editor, check when it was last updated (recently updated lists are the live ones), and pitch an inclusion with a short differentiator plus proof (data, award, review score). Ask for a high position, because position correlates with recommendation.
- Pay-to-play lists, such as "sponsored" listicle networks and affiliate lists that charge for inclusion: there is no evidence either way on how AI engines weight them. Paid inclusion without a clear label risks FTC endorsement-disclosure trouble, and it is a reputational risk if exposed. Treat them as a low priority and require disclosure.
- Publishing your own honest comparison or "best" page is supported for SaaS and agencies. For local businesses it matters less than getting onto third-party "best in [city]" lists.

### Gaps
- No controlled study shows that adding a brand to a list causes it to be added to AI answers. The evidence is correlation plus anecdote.
- No data was found on pay-to-play list prices or how often paid lists get cited.
- The Ahrefs study covers ChatGPT only. Listicle share in Gemini, Claude and Grok was not found.

## Reviews and review platforms

### Takeaway
Reviews matter, especially for local businesses (grade B: expert survey and vendor data), but the evidence that pure review *count* drives AI mentions is weak. G2's own study found a near-zero R² for review count. What matters more is being present on several authoritative review sites in your industry, having high ratings, and having positive review text. For SaaS, nearly every tool ChatGPT recommends has G2 and Capterra listings, so a listing there is the entry ticket rather than an edge. The FTC fake-review rule (in force since Oct 21 2024) makes any fake, bought or sentiment-conditioned review a civil-penalty risk.

### Cited Findings
- Whitespark 2026 AI-visibility factor scores: authority of the third-party sites holding your reviews 156 (#5); high Google star rating 146 (#8); variety of third-party review sites 136 (#11); positive sentiment in review text 126 (#22). Darren Shaw: "Get reviews on the prominent review sites in your industry. You can't just send review requests to Google anymore." — [Whitespark](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)
- Yext analyzed 6.8M citations across ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity (about 1.6M queries per model, Jul-Aug 2025; retail, financial services, healthcare and food service). First-party websites were 44% of citations, listings 42%, and reviews/social 8%. Branded or subjective queries leaned more on listings and reviews. OpenAI leaned toward listings (48.7%) and Gemini toward websites (52.1%). This is vendor research and Yext sells listings management. — [Yext blog](https://www.yext.com/blog/ai-citations-86-percent-of-sources-are-brand-managed); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251009106549/en/Yext-Research-86-of-AI-Citations-Come-from-Brand-Managed-Sources-Clarifying-How-Marketers-Can-Compete-in-the-AI-Search-Era)
- BrightLocal's 2026 consumer survey (as reported second-hand): 45% of US consumers used an AI tool for a local business recommendation in the past 12 months; 31% used ChatGPT and 23% Google AI Mode; 97% of AI users double-check AI recommendations against real reviews. BrightLocal's own tool page gives a different figure (58%). — [BrightLocal research](https://brightlocal.com/research); [BrightLocal tool page](https://www.brightlocal.com/local-seo-tools/local-ai-visibility)
- One agency claims businesses with strong review profiles are "3x more likely" to be cited by ChatGPT. This is unverified and grade C. A "150-review threshold" claim also circulates from an agency blog and is likewise unverified. — [PushLeads](https://pushleads.com/how-many-google-reviews-you-need-to-show-up-in-ai-search-the-150-review-threshol/)
- **SaaS:** G2's own analysis of about 30k citations found a "small but reliable" relationship between G2 review count and LLM citations, with an R² of about 0.009, which is very weak. — [G2](https://learn.g2.com/do-more-g2-reviews-mean-more-ai-visibility)
- Hall's analysis of 456,570 citations (May-Jun 2025) found G2 held only 8.25% of ChatGPT's B2B-software citations, behind GetApp. — [Hall](https://usehall.com/guides/review-platform-ai-citation-analysis)
- DerivateX (May 2026) found review sites were only 0.9% of citations when ChatGPT recommended B2B SaaS, with G2 and Capterra at zero. That conflicts with Hall, which shows how much the method changes the result. — [DerivateX](https://derivatex.agency/report/b2b-saas-ai-citation-study/)
- A secondary source reports that every B2B SaaS tool ChatGPT named had Capterra reviews and 99% had G2 reviews. — [Averi](https://www.averi.ai/how-to/get-your-saas-cited-on-g2-capterra-trustpilot-in-ai)
- **FTC rule:** finalized Aug 14 2024 and effective Oct 21 2024. It bans fake reviews, including AI-generated reviews and reviews by people with no real experience; paying or rewarding people for reviews of a particular sentiment; insider reviews without disclosure; review suppression through threats; and fake follower purchases. Civil penalties are about $51-52k per violation (inflation-adjusted). Liability covers firms that "knew or should have known." — [WSGR](https://www.wsgrdataadvisor.com/2024/08/ftc-issues-final-rule-banning-fake-and-misleading-consumer-reviews-and-testimonials/); [Freshfields](https://blog.freshfields.us/post/102jh9p/ftc-announces-final-rule-on-deceptive-reviews); [ETCentric](https://www.etcentric.org/ftc-rule-prohibiting-fake-and-paid-reviews-goes-into-effect/)

### Inferences
- Local to-dos: keep Google reviews coming at a steady pace (a rating floor plus recency) and add the 2-3 platforms that are authoritative for the vertical, such as Yelp, Tripadvisor, Healthgrades, Avvo or Houzz. Choose the platforms by checking which review domains the audit shows engines citing for that category.
- Ethical asks: ask every customer, not only the happy ones (asking only happy customers is "review gating", which Google prohibits). Use a post-service email or SMS with a direct link. Never make an incentive depend on a positive review. Never write reviews for customers or use AI to write them. Reply to reviews, because replies add fresh text on the page.
- SaaS to-dos: make sure the G2, Capterra and GetApp profiles exist, are complete and are categorized correctly. Push for enough reviews to rank in the category grid, because grid rank may matter more than raw count. The weak R² means review count alone is not a lever worth heavy spending.

### Gaps
- No study isolates review recency or review text as a cause of AI mentions.
- The BrightLocal primary report was not read directly, and figures differ between sources (45% vs 58%).
- Trustpilot and app-store effects: no reliable data found.

## Reddit, forums and Quora

### Takeaway
Reddit's citation share is large but very volatile. In ChatGPT it fell from about 60% of answers to about 10% (Aug-Sep 2025), and again by 86% in Aug 2026, and the Google surfaces dropped less. Google has a confirmed data-licensing deal with Reddit (about $60M a year, reported). The OpenAI partnership exists, but its terms have not been disclosed. In local-intent queries, forums are a small share (about 2% in Yext's data). The tactic should be genuine expert participation, not seeded posts. Astroturfing breaks Reddit rules, can trigger FTC endorsement issues when undisclosed, and tends to backfire.

### Cited Findings
- Semrush (more than 230k prompts, ChatGPT/AI Mode/Perplexity, Jul 14 to Oct 12 2025): ChatGPT cited Reddit in about 60% of responses in early Aug 2025, falling to about 10% by mid-Sep. The drop was specific to ChatGPT. Kevin Indig linked it to Google removing the num=100 parameter. — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/reddits-citations-in-chatgpt-fall/); [Semrush most-cited domains](https://www.semrush.com/blog/most-cited-domains-ai/)
- Semrush/Promptwatch (Aug 26 2026): Reddit's share of ChatGPT citations went from a steady 3.8% (Jul 18-Aug 7 2026) to 0.5% on Aug 14-17, a drop of 86%. Over the same period AI Mode fell 31% and AI Overviews 11%. Promptwatch calls the data provisional. OpenAI says it doesn't set fixed visibility levels for any site. — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/reddits-citations-in-chatgpt-fall/)
- A Semrush study of 325k prompts (Jan-Feb 2026) reportedly found Reddit to be the top-cited domain across ChatGPT Search, AI Mode and Perplexity. This was seen only through an eMarketer headline. — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/chart/c/368764)
- Profound (Aug 2024 to Jun 2025) put Reddit at 6.6% of Perplexity citations. The denominators differ from other studies, so the figures are not comparable. — [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/blog/ai-platform-citation-patterns)
- Yext (local, location-based queries): Reddit and similar forums were only about 2% of citations. — [Yext](https://www.yext.com/blog/ai-citations-86-percent-of-sources-are-brand-managed)
- The Google–Reddit deal was confirmed in Feb 2024 and gives Google access to Reddit's Data API, at about $60M a year per Bloomberg and Reuters. — [Engadget](https://engadget.com/reddit-is-licensing-its-content-to-google-to-help-train-its-ai-models-200013007.html); [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2024/02/22/reddit_google_license_ipo_altman/)
- OpenAI–Reddit: the terms were never disclosed. Search Engine Land estimated about $70M a year. — [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/openai-may-pay-reddit-70m-for-licensing-deal-451882)
- Ahrefs (via SEJ) reports that AI Mode leans on Wikipedia and Quora more than AI Overviews do. — [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-ai-overview/518427/)

### Inferences
- Reddit is a moving target for ChatGPT, so it should not be the sole off-site bet. It stays important for Google surfaces and Perplexity, which are also the engines with the licensing deal or the steadiest citations.
- Audit-driven to-do: for each Reddit or Quora thread URL cited where the client is absent, have a named, disclosed employee or founder account give a genuinely useful answer that states the affiliation. Owner or staff AMAs and a brand subreddit are fine. Buying aged accounts, upvote services or undisclosed "customer" posts is astroturfing: it breaks Reddit's content policy against manipulation, and undisclosed material connections fall under the FTC endorsement guides.
- Local businesses: city subreddits matter for some "best X in [city]" questions, but the Yext data suggests listings and websites dominate local citations, so Reddit is a lower priority there.

### Gaps
- No reliable share data for Reddit in Claude, Gemini or Grok. (Grok presumably leans on X, but no data was found.)
- No study measures whether a brand's own Reddit participation lifts AI mentions.
- Reddit's specific self-promotion rules (e.g. the "10% rule") were not verified this session.

## YouTube, LinkedIn, Wikipedia/Wikidata, digital PR, podcasts, and brand mentions vs backlinks

### Takeaway
The strongest single correlation is unlinked brand mentions across the web (Spearman 0.664 with AI Overview visibility, versus 0.218 for backlinks; Ahrefs, 75k brands, grade A/B). YouTube is the most-cited domain in Google AI Overviews, but other engines rarely cite it. Digital PR shows up in AI answers within days to weeks, but each citation decays in about 3-6 weeks, so steady coverage beats one-off hits. No reliable data was found for LinkedIn or podcasts. Wikipedia is heavily cited, but paid or conflict-of-interest editing breaks its rules.

### Cited Findings
- Ahrefs (May 2025, 75k brands, AI Overviews, Spearman): branded web mentions 0.664, branded anchors 0.527, branded search volume 0.392, DR 0.326, referring domains 0.295, backlinks 0.218, site page count 0.17. The strongest factors were all off-site. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/)
- A Medium write-up summarizes this as "mentions beat backlinks 3-to-1". That is the writer's framing, not Ahrefs'. — [Medium](https://medium.com/@lorenzoswanson786/ahrefs-studied-75-000-brands-brand-mentions-beat-backlinks-3-to-1-for-ai-visibility-e86ef9f6baba)
- Whitespark (local): number of unlinked brand mentions scored 135 (#12) on AI visibility. Darren Shaw: "In AI SEO, mentions (citations) are the new link." — [Whitespark](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)
- YouTube is reportedly the most-cited domain in AI Overviews per Ahrefs Brand Radar, up 34% in six months. In a study of 863k keywords and 4M AIO URLs, YouTube was 5.6% of all AIO citations and 18.2% of citations from pages outside the top 100. Gemini and Copilot reportedly barely cite YouTube. — [SEJ](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-ai-overview/518427/); [Radiant Elephant](https://www.radiantelephant.com/youtube-ai-overview-citations/); [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-citations-top-10)
- AI Mode and AI Overviews reportedly overlap on only 13.7% of the sources they cite, per Ahrefs data via a second-hand source (grade C). — [Quattr](https://www.quattr.com/blog/takeaway-from-ahrefs-ai-search-study)
- Ahrefs: AI assistants linked to Ahrefs in only 28% of the answers that mentioned it. Mentions and citations are different metrics. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-citations-vs-impressions-study/)
- Scrunch (3.5M citation events): ChatGPT replaces its sources fastest, with a citation half-life of 3.4 weeks; Perplexity's is 5.8 weeks. Citations from Stacker-network domains lasted about 2x longer. Scrunch is a vendor. — [Scrunch](https://scrunch.com/blog/half-life-of-ai-citations)
- Wikipedia's AI Overviews and AI Mode share held steady in 2025 while Reddit's fell in ChatGPT. — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/blog/reddits-citations-in-chatgpt-fall/)
- Wikipedia's rules require paid editors to disclose who pays them, under the Wikimedia Terms of Use and the paid-contribution disclosure policy. Its conflict-of-interest guideline discourages editing articles about yourself or your employer directly and says to propose changes on the talk page instead. — [Wikipedia: Paid-contribution disclosure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Paid-contribution_disclosure); [Wikipedia: Conflict of interest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Conflict_of_interest) (policy pages cited from standing knowledge; not re-fetched this session)

### Inferences
- PR to-dos: aim for *mention volume on relevant domains* (trade press, local news, industry sites), not link counts. Use the "cited news/editorial URLs" in the audit as the pitch list. Plan coverage monthly, because citations decay in weeks.
- YouTube: make it a to-do when the audit shows youtube.com URLs cited in AI Overviews for the client's queries. Use explainer, how-to and comparison videos with the brand named in the title and description and accurate transcripts.
- Wikipedia/Wikidata: only for brands that already meet notability through independent coverage. Make a disclosed talk-page request, or create or fix the Wikidata item (official site, founding date, identifiers). Never hire undisclosed paid editors.
- LinkedIn and podcasts: treat these as mention generators (founder posts, guest appearances with show notes that name the brand), not proven citation sources.

### Gaps
- No reliable data on LinkedIn's citation share or podcast impact. Podcast pages are rarely tracked as a separate category.
- The Ahrefs mention correlation covers AI Overviews only. Equivalent studies for ChatGPT, Claude and Grok were not found.
- Grok's sources (likely X posts) were not researched with data.

## Directories and listings consistency

### Takeaway
For local businesses, listings are a huge share of AI citations (42% in Yext's data, and 48.7% for OpenAI). But Whitespark's experts rank *unstructured* citations (mentions on news, blogs and industry sites) well above structured directory volume and NAP consistency (name, address, phone) for AI visibility. Consistency across Google, Bing and Apple is a baseline task, not a growth lever. For SaaS, the listing set is G2, Capterra, GetApp, Product Hunt and Crunchbase. Presence there is the entry ticket.

### Cited Findings
- Yext: listings were 42% of AI citations in local/brand queries, and OpenAI leaned on listings for 48.7%. Perplexity spread citations across sites such as MapQuest and Tripadvisor. — [Yext](https://www.yext.com/blog/ai-citations-86-percent-of-sources-are-brand-managed)
- Whitespark AI scores: quality of unstructured citations 160 (#4); quantity of unstructured citations 147 (#7); citations from industry-relevant domains 133 (#15); quality of structured citations 123 (#26); consistency across Google, Bing and Apple Maps 116 (#31); number of structured citations (directories, data aggregators) 106 (#40). — [Whitespark](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/)
- SaaS: Hall found GetApp ahead of G2 in ChatGPT B2B-software citations (G2 at 8.25%). — [Hall](https://usehall.com/guides/review-platform-ai-citation-analysis)

### Inferences
- Bing Places matters because ChatGPT search has historically used Bing's index, and Apple Business Connect feeds Siri and Apple Maps. Both are cheap one-time tasks. No study measuring their AI impact was found.
- Prioritize the directories that appear in audit-cited URLs for the category over bulk citation-building services.

### Gaps
- No direct evidence on how much Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Crunchbase or Product Hunt each lift AI mentions.
- Whether ChatGPT still relies mainly on Bing in 2026 was not verified this session.

## Using the engines' cited URLs as the target list ("citation gap" analysis)

### Takeaway
The leading tools already productize this. Profound's "Citation Gap Analysis" finds prompts where competitors are cited and you aren't. It also tracks each cited URL's first citation, peak and half-life. Engines overlap little in what they cite (about 11% between ChatGPT and Perplexity, and 13.7% between AI Mode and AI Overviews), so gap lists must be built per engine. Ahrefs data shows more than half of cited sources are third-party pages, which is exactly the outreach list.

### Cited Findings
- Profound Citation Gap Analysis agent (via Optimizely Opal): finds prompts where competitors are cited and you are not, shows share of voice by platform, and suggests 5-8 content topics. — [Optimizely support](https://support.optimizely.com/hc/en-us/articles/42860418745229)
- Profound's "Citation Decay" feature tracks each URL's first citation, growth to peak, peak volume and half-life. — [Discovered Labs review](https://discoveredlabs.com/blog/profound-ai-visibility-tool-review) (vendor-affiliated, grade C)
- Reviewers note that Profound diagnoses but does not tell you what to change next week, so execution falls to the agency. — [Discovered Labs](https://discoveredlabs.com/blog/profound-ai-visibility-tool-review); [The Rank Masters](https://www.therankmasters.com/insights/ai-visibility/ai-citation-gap-analysis-tools)
- About 11% citation overlap between ChatGPT and Perplexity was reported (second-hand). — [The Rank Masters](https://www.therankmasters.com/insights/ai-visibility/ai-citation-gap-analysis-tools)
- Ahrefs Brand Radar exposes "Cited domains / Cited pages" per prompt set, which is the raw material for a target list. — [Ahrefs Academy](https://ahrefs.com/academy/how-to-use-brand-radar/cited-domains)
- DerivateX: when ChatGPT recommends a SaaS tool, it cites that tool's own site only 11.6% of the time, so most of what drives the recommendation lives off-site. — [DerivateX](https://derivatex.agency/report/b2b-saas-ai-citation-study/)

### Inferences
- A pipeline the product can automate: for each prompt where the client is absent, take the cited URLs → remove duplicates across prompts and engines → weight each URL by how many prompts and engines cite it → classify it (listicle / review platform / directory / forum thread / video / news / Wikipedia / competitor site) → map each class to a to-do template:
  - **Listicle:** pitch the author for inclusion.
  - **Review platform:** claim the profile and run a review drive.
  - **Directory:** create or fix the listing.
  - **Forum thread:** have a disclosed expert reply.
  - **Video:** make a video or seek a creator collab.
  - **News:** pitch the outlet.
  - **Competitor's own page:** no outreach; build a comparison page instead.
- Flag URLs updated recently as "live". Ahrefs found 26% of cited lists were updated in the past two months, so those editors are actively maintaining them.

### Gaps
- No published success rate for citation-gap outreach, such as the percentage of pitched listicles that add the brand.

## Typical timelines from placement to change in AI answers

### Takeaway
No controlled before/after study was found. Vendor estimates: Perplexity picks up a new placement in about 1-7 days, ChatGPT search in about 3-14 days, and AI Overviews in about 2-3 weeks. Most campaigns see citation activity within 2-4 weeks. Citations decay with a half-life of about 3-6 weeks, so keep placements fresh. Changes to the models' built-in (training) knowledge, with no web search, take a model-release cycle, often months.

### Cited Findings
- Agency estimate: Perplexity 1-7 days, ChatGPT with search 3-14 days, first AI Overviews citation 2-3 weeks after a strong placement. This is an estimate, not a measurement (grade C). — [AuthorityTech](https://authoritytech.io/blog/earned-media-ai-citation-timeline-2026)
- "Most campaigns see citation activity within 2-4 weeks of placement going live"; new or low-authority sources take longer (grade C). — [AuthorityTech](https://authoritytech.io/curated/how-to-run-pr-campaigns-ai-search-citations-2026)
- Citation half-life is 3.4 weeks for ChatGPT and 5.8 weeks for Perplexity (Scrunch, 3.5M events, grade B). — [Scrunch](https://scrunch.com/blog/half-life-of-ai-citations)
- A vendor claims 17% of citations of press releases occurred within 24 hours (method unverified, grade C); another source claims "89% of AI visibility wins are gone within a month". — [Everything PR](https://everything-pr.com/the-citation-half-life-why-89-percent-of-ai-visibility-wins-are-gone-within-a-month)
- Ahrefs: 79.1% of cited lists were updated in 2025, which suggests engines prefer fresh pages. — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/best-lists-research/)

### Inferences
- Set client expectations: first signs in 2-4 weeks on engines that search the web; measure on a weekly scan cadence; judge each tactic over 6-8 weeks. Training-data effects (Claude or ChatGPT answers without browsing) lag by months and can't be promised.
- The steady decay argues for recurring to-dos ("refresh/re-pitch every 4-6 weeks") rather than one-off tasks.

### Gaps
- No independent, controlled timeline study was found. Every timeline figure is a vendor estimate or vendor-measured.
- No data on how fast Claude, Gemini or Grok answers change.

## Local vs SaaS split (summary for the report writer)

### Takeaway
Local priority order (by Whitespark's AI scores and Yext's citation mix): curated "best in city" lists → prominence on industry and local news sites (unstructured citations) → reviews on Google plus the vertical's authoritative review sites → listings and NAP consistency on Google, Bing and Apple as the baseline. SaaS priority order (Ahrefs, Hall, DerivateX): third-party "best X" listicles and comparison pages → brand mentions and digital PR → G2, Capterra and GetApp presence and grid rank → Reddit and YouTube where the audit shows they are cited → your own honest comparison pages.

### Cited Findings
- Local factor scores — [Whitespark](https://whitespark.ca/local-search-ranking-factors/); local citation mix — [Yext](https://www.yext.com/blog/ai-citations-86-percent-of-sources-are-brand-managed)
- SaaS listicle and source data — [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/best-lists-research/); [Hall](https://usehall.com/guides/review-platform-ai-citation-analysis); [DerivateX](https://derivatex.agency/report/b2b-saas-ai-citation-study/)

### Inferences
- Ethical and terms-of-service red lines to bake into the product's to-dos: no fake or AI-written reviews and no rewards that depend on sentiment (FTC rule, about $51k+ per violation); no review gating; no undisclosed Reddit or Quora posting or vote manipulation; no undisclosed paid Wikipedia edits; paid listicle placements must carry a disclosure.

### Gaps
- The local and SaaS orderings are synthesized from different studies with different methods. No single study ranks every tactic head to head.
