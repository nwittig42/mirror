## 4. Objection one-liners

- **"My SEO agency handles this."** → "Ask them your ChatGPT visibility score. Different channel, different rules, 89% of what Perplexity cites isn't what ChatGPT cites, and neither is your Google ranking. We run alongside them; we don't replace them."
- **"Is anyone actually finding med spas on ChatGPT?"** → *(don't argue stats, open the live answer)* "Someone is being chosen from this answer right now. It isn't you."
- **"I can check ChatGPT myself."** → "Once, sure. We run 40 checks a week across four engines, on the same prompts, and catch changes within days. It's monitoring, not a search."
- **"Too expensive."** → "One retained filler patient is worth $15–25K over their lifetime. This is $[X]/mo. If it brings you one patient a year it's paid for itself several times."
- **"How do I know it works?"** → "You'll see it: every number in your monthly report traces to a logged check, verbatim answers, dated screenshots, before and after. And your baseline audit is free, so you'll know exactly where you start."
- **"Let me think about it."** → "Take the audit either way. It's yours. Founding-client pricing ([$495/$995]) is locked for life for the first five practices; after that it goes up. That part I can't hold."

## 6. 15-minute call script (skeleton)

1. **(2 min)** "Before I show anything, when a new patient books, how do they usually say they found you?" *(listen; you'll reuse their words)*
2. **(5 min)** Screen-share the worst verbatim answer. Silence. Let them read it. Then: "A patient asking that question sees this."
3. **(3 min)** The score + competitor leaderboard. "This is measurable, and it moves."
4. **(3 min)** The plan: "Month one: fact sheet, listings rebuild, credential chain, first fixes. Every week you get an email with what AI said about you, verbatim. Every month, the report: what changed, what we fixed, what's next."
5. **(2 min)** Close: "Founding rate is [$995] locked for life, month to month, cancel anytime. If the baseline audit hadn't already found at least three real issues, I wouldn't be asking. Want to start this week?"
