# Pricing + Delivery Playbook
## GEO Service for LA Aesthetic Practices, built on the VentureCite loop

*Companion to the research report. Pricing follows the Van Westendorp / Hormozi / ProfitWell framework; delivery maps the VentureCite Platform Guide loop (Set up → Monitor → Diagnose → Act → Report) onto a solo-operator service.*

---

# PART 1: THE PRICE

## Goal assumption

You're at **market entry**: zero case studies, need first 3–5 paying clients fast, but in a vertical where price signals quality (aesthetic medicine is premium-coded; these owners charge $14/unit for Botox partly *because* it's not $9). That rules out bargain pricing even at entry. Optimizing for: market entry now, LTV later.

## Why $300–500 is wrong as the headline price

- The **software alone** (Otterly Premium $489, Goodie $495, Profound $499) costs that with zero human service. Pricing done-for-you at software prices tells buyers it's software.
- Their reference prices: local SEO $1,500–2,500/mo, PatientGain $1,699–2,499/mo, social $300–3,000/mo. At $400/mo you'd be priced like a social-media intern.
- Hormozi rule: in a premium vertical, underpricing *costs* you sales; the owner thinks "what's the catch."
- Your unit economics: one retained filler patient ≈ $15–25K lifetime. The ROI story works at any price under $2K/mo; use it.

## The tier structure

Value metric: **flat per brand/location per month** (multi-location = per location, 2nd+ at 40% off). Anchor the top tier first, per Hormozi; the middle tier is where you want most buyers to land.

| | **WATCH** | **FIX** ← flagship | **DOMINATE** (anchor) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | **$495/mo** | **$995/mo** | **$1,995/mo** |
| Weekly AI scans across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude (10 tracked prompts) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ (20 prompts) |
| Fact Sheet built + maintained (ground truth) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Hallucination detection + critical alerts within 48h | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ (24h) |
| Monthly PDF report (score, trend, per-engine, verbatim answers) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Competitor leaderboard (3 tracked) | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ (6 tracked) |
| **Fixes executed monthly** (GBP/Yelp/Bing/listings, credential corrections, review strategy) | N/A | ✓ 3/mo | ✓ unlimited from worklist |
| **1 cited-content piece/mo** (treatment page or FAQ set, fact-sheet-grounded) | N/A | ✓ | ✓ 3/mo |
| Listicle discovery + outreach ("best med spa in X" placements) | N/A | ✓ 2 targets/mo | ✓ campaign |
| Quarterly strategy call | N/A | ✓ | ✓ monthly |
| Reddit/community monitoring + engagement plan | N/A | N/A | ✓ |
| Multi-provider credential audit (per-injector visibility) | N/A | N/A | ✓ |

**Forcing functions** (why each upgrade happens):
- Watch → Fix: Watch *finds* problems but doesn't fix them. Every Watch report ends with the worklist they're not getting done. This is deliberate. Watch is a conveyor belt into Fix.
- Fix → Dominate: competitor pressure. When the report shows a named competitor winning 60% of "best of" prompts, Dominate is the "we take the whole neighborhood" answer. Also the only tier for 3+ injector practices and multi-location.

**Structure checks:** top = 2x middle ✓ (within 2–3x rule). Top vs. lowest paid = 4x ✓ (under the 5x defection ceiling). Middle sits just under their local-SEO reference price, reads as "serious but not another $2K retainer."

## Onboarding fee

**$495 one-time setup** (fact sheet build, crawler audit, baseline citation check, kickoff call), **waived if they start on Fix or higher, or prepay a quarter.** The fee exists mostly to be waived: it makes the flagship feel like a deal and filters tire-kickers on Watch.

## Trial / discount / terms

- **No free trial.** Services don't trial. The free **baseline audit** (the 3-practice pitch asset from the action plan) is the trial. They've already felt the value before the first invoice.
- **Founding-client rate:** first 5 clients get these prices **locked for life** + named in your future case studies. This is your urgency lever instead of discounting. After 5, list prices go to $595/$1,195/$2,495 (announce this in the pitch; it's true and it forces the close).
- **Annual:** don't lead with it (no track record = annual is a hard ask). Offer **quarterly prepay at 10% off** for cash flow. Introduce annual at 2-months-free (17%) once you have a 6-month case study.
- **Grandfathering:** founding clients indefinite; everyone else, 60-day notice on increases.
- **Risk reversal (value stack, costs you nothing):** "If the baseline audit doesn't surface at least 3 material findings, inaccuracies, missed citations, or competitor gaps. You don't pay setup and we part friends." You will never lose this bet; the research shows every unmanaged practice has findings.

## Validation + kill criteria

Run informal Van Westendorp in your first 10 pitches, literally ask two of the four questions on calls: *"At what monthly price would this be so expensive you'd pass?"* and *"What would feel like a bargain for someone checking what AI says about you every week?"* Log answers; 10 data points beats guessing.

**Falsifiable kill criteria (60 days):**
1. If fewer than 1 in 10 pitched practices converts at $995, drop Fix to $795, don't touch Watch (it's already at the software floor).
2. If everyone buys Watch and nobody upgrades within 60 days, the Fix forcing function is broken, worklists aren't painful enough or reports aren't landing. Fix the report before the price.
3. If a founding client churns before month 4, treat it as a delivery-value failure, not pricing, do the exit interview before changing anything.

**Bottom line: $995/mo flagship, $495 monitoring floor, $1,995 anchor, $495 setup (usually waived), founding-5 lock, quarterly prepay 10%.** Three Fix clients ≈ $3K MRR at roughly 15 hrs/mo of your time.

# PART 2: THE DELIVERY SYSTEM

The VentureCite loop is the client experience. Your job as operator: run the loop, and make every stage *visible* to the client. The core perceived-value principle: **work the client can't see doesn't exist.** Every stage below has (a) what you actually do, and (b) what the client sees.

## Stage 0: SET UP (week 1, once per client)

**What you do (~3–4 hrs):**
1. Paste their website into VentureCite → confirm/correct the auto-built brand profile. Add **name variations** ("Dr. Sarah Kim," "Kim Dermatology," "SK Derm"), med spas get cited under provider names as often as brand names.
2. Build the **Fact Sheet**: providers + verified credentials (CA medical/nursing board lookup), every service with brand names (Botox vs. Dysport vs. Jeuveau), pricing, hours, the NOT-offered list (hallucination bait), compliance red lines. Resolve scrape-vs-entered conflicts. Keep 3+ facts active minimum (hallucination detector requirement); realistically you'll have 25–40.
3. Run **Crawler** check, 15+ AI bots against their site. Med spa sites are notorious for JS-heavy templates and blocked bots; this frequently finds a real problem on day one. Send the robots.txt fix to their web person.
4. Write the **10 tracked prompts** from the research report's battery (neighborhood + hero treatments + branded fact-checks), replacing the auto-generated ones where needed.
5. Run the first citation check.

**What the client sees:** a 30-minute kickoff call where you walk them through their own Fact Sheet and make *them* approve each fact ("is your Botox still $13/unit?"). This does double duty: it's genuinely necessary for ground truth, and it's the moment they internalize that someone is now formally responsible for what AI says about them. End the call by showing one verbatim AI answer about their practice, right or wrong, it lands.

## Stage 1: MONITOR (automated weekly; your time ~15 min/client)

**What you do:** scans run automatically. You skim the Command Center: score movement, cited checks, new competitor appearances, Reddit mentions. Flag anything for Diagnose.

**What the client sees: the Weekly Pulse email (this is the #1 perceived-value asset, ~10 min to send):**

> **Subject: What AI told patients about [Practice] this week**
> - Visibility score: 38 → 42 (+4)
> - This week ChatGPT answered "best med spa in Culver City for Botox". You were **named 2nd**, behind [Competitor]. Verbatim: *"...[Practice] is known for natural-looking results..."*
> - Perplexity still misses you on lip filler prompts (fix in progress, Yelp services update went live Tuesday)
> - No new inaccuracies detected across 40 checks ✓
> - Next up: [one line]

Rules: always include one **verbatim quote** (nothing else you send is as compelling as the AI's own words about them), always show the check count ("40 checks" = scale of work), always name a competitor (keeps the pressure alive), never longer than 6 lines. Templatize it; fill from the dashboard.

## Stage 2: DIAGNOSE (weekly skim + event-driven)

**What you do:** review Hallucinations tab against the Fact Sheet (severity-filtered), run Signals on any page you're about to optimize, re-run Crawler monthly.

**What the client sees:** the **Critical Alert**, when an engine states something false about credentials, pricing, or services, they get a same-day standalone email: side-by-side "what the AI said vs. the truth," which platform, severity, and *what you're doing about it*. This is the moment they'll forward to their business partner. It's also your compliance-hook promise ("we make sure AI doesn't say something false about your practice") being visibly kept. Watch-tier clients get the alert + DIY fix steps; Fix-tier clients get "already handling it."

## Stage 3: ACT (the monthly fix block, ~4–6 hrs/client on Fix tier)

Priority order per the research (impact ÷ effort for this niche):

1. **Listings block (month 1):** GBP category/services/Q&A, **Yelp completeness** (direct Perplexity feed; 3.4× local AI citations), Bing Places, RealSelf profile claim, credential consistency across state board/Healthgrades/RealSelf. Mostly one-time; huge report fodder.
2. **Content (every month):** one fact-sheet-grounded piece via Create → Signals score → Chunk Engineer → publish on their site. Alternate: treatment-page rewrite with LA-specific pricing/FAQ (months 1, 3, 5...), FAQ batch + FAQPage schema via the FAQ tab (months 2, 4...). Grounding in the Fact Sheet means you can safely promise "nothing made up", say that to the client, it matters in a medical practice.
3. **Listicles (every month, 2 targets):** harvest the actual cited URLs from citation checks → GEO Assets › Listicles for discovery/outreach tracking → pitch inclusion. One landed listicle typically moves more "best of" prompts than everything else combined; when one lands, it's the lead item in that month's report.
4. **Reviews (standing item):** monthly nudge to the client, "this month, ask happy patients to mention *the treatment and provider by name*." Costs you nothing, compounds, and its effect shows up in *their* review count, which you then report as progress.
5. **Community (Dominate only):** monitor subreddit mentions; engage only where authentic. Never astroturf a medical brand. One called-out fake post in r/30PlusSkinCare does more damage than a year of GEO fixes. Skip Wikipedia for single practices (notability), despite the platform offering it.

**What the client sees:** every action logged in a running **Activity Log** (a shared doc or the report appendix). Itemize atomically: "Updated 14 GBP service entries" not "updated GBP." A month of Fix-tier work itemizes to 15–25 line items. This log is the answer to "what am I paying for?" before it's ever asked.

## Stage 4: REPORT (monthly, ~1 hr/client)

Export the VentureCite report PDF (score, trend, per-engine breakdown, real measured checks, no estimates) and wrap it in one page of yours:

1. **Plain-English verdict** (top, 2 sentences): "AI engines now name you in 6 of 10 patient questions, up from 2 in January. Perplexity is your strongest platform; Gemini is next quarter's focus."
2. **Accuracy section:** inaccuracies found/fixed/still open, *this is the section no SEO agency can show them* and the reason they don't churn.
3. **What we did**: the itemized Activity Log.
4. **What's next**: 3 items from the Worklist (sets up next month's value in advance).
5. Screenshot of the single best verbatim answer of the month.

**Quarterly (Fix+):** 20-minute call walking the trend charts. Trends over 90 days is where "is this working?" gets answered visually, score up and competitor share-of-voice donut shifting is the renewal conversation happening by itself.

## The perceived-value system, summarized

| Touchpoint | Cadence | Your time | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly Pulse email | Weekly | 10 min | Verbatim AI quotes + check counts = constant visible vigilance |
| Critical Alert | Event-driven | 20 min | Same-day vigilance on the thing they fear most; the forwardable email |
| Activity Log | Continuous | 2 min/action | Answers "what am I paying for" preemptively, atomically |
| Monthly report | Monthly | 1 hr | Measured numbers + accuracy section nobody else offers |
| Quarterly trend call | Quarterly | 30 min | Renewal insurance via 90-day charts |
| Fact Sheet approval | Onboarding + on change | N/A | Makes *them* co-own the ground truth; switching cost |

Total steady-state load per Fix client: **~8–10 hrs/mo** → at $995, ~$100–125/hr effective, with automation (scans, alerts template, report export) pushing that up over time. Watch clients: ~2 hrs/mo.

One honest caveat to keep in your head (not the client's inbox): weekly scans of 10 prompts are a *sample*, not the universe, AI answers are non-deterministic and personalized. Never promise "AI will always recommend you"; promise "we watch, we catch, we fix, and here's the measured trend." Every claim in the reports stays defensible because every number comes from a logged check.
